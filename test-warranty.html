Warranty

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Overview

A.Okay! products are designed for long-term use, maintenance, and repair. We stand behind our work and want to help when something goes wrong.

Warranty coverage

All current A.Okay! products include a one-year limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. This includes MID.1, MID.1.LID, MID.1.PET, and separately purchased replacement parts and accessories.

Coverage begins on the date of delivery and continues for one year.

This warranty covers the product and its original components supplied by A.Okay!. It extends to subsequent consumer owners for the remainder of the original warranty period. Transferring ownership does not restart or extend coverage.

Future A.Okay! products may have different warranty terms, which will be specified on their product pages at the time of purchase.

What's covered

This warranty covers defects in materials, manufacturing, or assembly that affect a product's intended appearance, construction, or function.

Examples include defective electronic components, faulty electrical connections, manufacturing defects in structural parts, and defects in materials or finishes resulting from production.

Coverage includes qualifying defects in components supplied by A.Okay!, even when those components are manufactured by third parties.

What's not covered

This warranty does not cover:

  • Normal wear and tear, including cosmetic wear resulting from ordinary use.
  • Accidental damage, misuse, neglect, or improper storage.
  • Damage caused by improper installation, assembly, or operation.
  • Damage resulting from liquid exposure, excessive force, or use outside a product's intended conditions.
  • Defects in third-party components not supplied by A.Okay!.
  • Damage or malfunctions caused by modifications, incompatible components, or improper repairs.

Disassembling a product, performing maintenance, installing compatible components, or using independent repair services does not automatically void this warranty.

Coverage may be denied for defects or damage caused by these activities, but unrelated defects remain covered.

Repairs, replacements, and refunds

If a covered product develops a qualifying defect during the warranty period, we'll work with you to resolve it.

Depending on the issue, we may provide replacement parts, repair the affected product or component, or replace it. If repair or replacement is not reasonably available, we may provide a refund, subject to applicable law and your agreement where required.

Whenever practical, we prefer repair over replacement.

There is no charge for parts or labor necessary to remedy an approved warranty defect. A.Okay! will also cover reasonable shipping expenses associated with approved warranty service.

We'll provide instructions or arrange an appropriate shipping method before asking you to return a product or component.

Repaired or replaced products and components remain covered for the remainder of the original warranty period, or any longer period required by applicable law.

How to request warranty service

To request warranty service, contact us at hello@aokay.cool with:

  • Your order number or other proof of purchase, if available.
  • A description of the problem.
  • Photographs or video, if helpful.

We'll review the issue, help with troubleshooting where appropriate, and explain the next steps.

Some issues may be resolved through replacement parts or repair instructions without requiring the entire product to be returned.

Please don't ship any products or components to us before receiving instructions.

Warranty service is provided or coordinated directly by A.Okay!. No product registration is required, and no mandatory third-party dispute resolution process applies.

Other rights

This limited warranty is provided in addition to any rights or remedies available under applicable consumer protection laws. It does not exclude or limit rights that cannot legally be waived.

This warranty does not impose a contractual limitation on the duration of applicable implied warranties.

This warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may also have other rights which vary from State to State.
