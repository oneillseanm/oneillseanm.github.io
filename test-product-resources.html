RAP (Replace a Part) program

MID.1 is designed for serviceability, so replacement parts are available individually if you need them.

If you lose a screw, wear out a rubber part, damage a cable, or need another part, get in touch and we'll help identify the part you need.

Replacement parts are priced according to the schedule below. Orders under $5 are rounded up to a $5 minimum parts charge, plus actual shipping.

Availability may vary over time, but we'll do our best to keep common service parts on hand and help with sourcing when we can.
