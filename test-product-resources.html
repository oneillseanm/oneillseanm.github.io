RAP (Replace A Part) program

Our products are designed for serviceability, so replacement parts are available individually if you need them.

If you lose a screw, wear out a rubber part, damage a cable, or need another part, email us at hello@aokay.cool and we'll help identify the parts you need and get them to you.

Replacement parts are priced according to the schedule below. Orders under $5 are rounded up to a $5 minimum parts charge, plus actual shipping.

Availability may vary over time, but we'll do our best to keep common service parts on hand.
