The custom MID.1 top case, available in two formats.

Additional top cases can replace an existing top case or let you to switch between different layouts.

The standard top case accommodates four full 12-unit rows of keys and is compatible with both the staggered and ortholinear PCBs.

The corner blocker top case adds 1.5-unit blockers at the bottom corners and is compatible with the staggered PCB only.
