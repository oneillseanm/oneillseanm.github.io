Shipping

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Order processing

A.Okay! ships from Seattle, Washington.

In-stock orders are typically processed and shipped within 5 business days, excluding weekends and holidays. Orders placed on weekends or holidays begin processing on the next business day.

If we anticipate a delay beyond the stated processing time, we'll contact you with an update and available options.

Shipping methods and costs

We offer tracked domestic and international shipping.

Available shipping methods, costs, and estimated delivery times are calculated and displayed at checkout based on your location and order details.

Delivery estimates begin once your order ships and do not include processing time. Actual delivery times may vary due to carrier delays, weather, customs processing, or other circumstances outside our control.

International shipping

We ship internationally to supported destinations. Shipping availability and costs are displayed at checkout.

International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, VAT, and carrier handling or brokerage fees. Unless explicitly stated otherwise at checkout, these charges are not included in your order total and are the customer's responsibility.

Customs processing may cause additional delivery delays.

Order tracking

You'll receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information when your order ships.

Tracking information may take some time to become available after the shipping confirmation is sent.

Shipping addresses

Please check your shipping address carefully before placing an order.

If you need to correct an address, contact us at hello@aokay.cool as soon as possible. We can make changes before shipment where possible, but cannot guarantee address changes once an order is in transit.

Lost, damaged, or delayed shipments

If your order arrives damaged, is missing items, or appears to have been lost in transit, please contact us at hello@aokay.cool.

We'll help investigate the issue with the shipping carrier and arrange an appropriate resolution, including repair, replacement, or refund where applicable.

For damaged deliveries, please retain the product and packaging until we've had an opportunity to review the issue.

Order changes and cancellations

Orders can be canceled or modified before shipment.

To request a change or cancellation, use your customer account or contact us at hello@aokay.cool.

Once an order has shipped, please refer to our Return & Refund Policy.

Contact

For shipping questions or assistance with an order, contact us at hello@aokay.cool.
