About

We make objects we genuinely want to own.

We're a small, independent design studio based in Seattle. We're drawn to useful objects with straightforward construction and follow our interests wherever they take us.

Sean O'Neill

Sean is a licensed architect who runs a solo residential architecture studio designing houses, cabins, tiny houses, and remodels. Some of his work has been featured in Dwell and a couple coffee table books.

flo truong

flo is a designer whose career has spanned agencies, independent digital product studios, and research. She currently works in accessibility design, focusing on making digital products more inclusive and usable.

Together

A.Okay! began as Sean's side project and has become something we're building together. It brings together our shared interests, along with the different perspectives each of us brings to the work.
