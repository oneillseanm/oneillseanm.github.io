I like to imagine what keyboards might have become if they’d stayed the sort of thing we think of as equipment. If they never became Keyboards, but remained mostly utilitarian devices designed by the same folks behind, say, your stereo receiver, the desk lamp in your workshop, or some control panel.

They’d probably make a keyboard from the same sorts of materials. Sheet metal, for one. They’d also give it a straightforward and descriptive, if slightly awkward, name. And, if nothing else, it would be dependable, durable, and repairable. What else does equipment need to be, really?

Keycaps, switches, and stabilizers are not included with MID.1. It is shown wearing PBS Black on Black keycaps, by Keyreative. A fine set, and arguably the chef's kiss on MID.1.
