Returns

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Returns and cancellations

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery.

Products may be carefully assembled and tested, provided they are returned complete, in like-new condition, and without damage or significant wear.

We strongly encourage returning products in their original packaging, with all included components, hardware, and accessories. Products must be packaged securely to prevent damage during return shipping.

We may decline returns or reduce refunds for products that are damaged, incomplete, or show signs of handling beyond what is necessary to evaluate them, where permitted by applicable law. Missing or damaged packaging may also result in a reduced refund if it causes a demonstrable loss in the product's value, where permitted by law.

For change-of-mind returns, customers are responsible for arranging and paying for return shipping. Original shipping charges are nonrefundable, except where required by law.

Orders may be canceled or modified before shipment.

How to start a return or cancellation

You can request a return or cancellation through your customer account or by contacting us at hello@aokay.cool.

If your return is approved, we'll provide instructions on how and where to send your item. Please don't send items back before receiving return instructions, except where applicable law provides otherwise.

Cancellation requests for orders that haven't shipped can also be submitted through your customer account or by email.

Damages and issues

Please inspect your order when it arrives and contact us promptly if anything is damaged, defective, missing, or incorrect.

We'll work with you to resolve the issue through repair, replacement, or refund, as appropriate. For defective products or fulfillment errors, we'll cover reasonable shipping costs associated with the remedy, where applicable.

Exceptions and non-returnable items

Certain products, including specially commissioned or customized items, may be designated final sale. Any such exceptions will be clearly identified before purchase and remain subject to applicable consumer protection laws.

Please contact us if you're unsure whether an item is eligible for return.

Exchanges

If you'd like to exchange an item, please contact us before sending it back. We'll help determine what options are available.

European Union returns and cancellations

If you are a consumer in the European Union and purchase goods online, you may have the right to cancel your order within 14 days after the day you receive your goods for any reason, unless an exemption applies.

After you notify us that you wish to cancel, you must return the goods within 14 days.

We will refund eligible canceled orders within 14 days after we are informed of your cancellation, but we may withhold the refund until we receive the goods back or receive evidence that you have sent them back.

For eligible cancellations, we will refund the product price and the cost of standard delivery, if you paid for delivery. We are not required to refund any additional amount paid for express or premium delivery beyond the cost of standard delivery.

To request a return or cancellation, follow the instructions above under "How to start a return or cancellation."

United Kingdom returns and cancellations

If you are a consumer in the United Kingdom and purchase goods online, you may have the right to cancel your order within 14 days after the day you receive your goods, unless an exemption applies.

After you tell us you wish to cancel, you must return the goods to us within 14 days.

We will refund eligible canceled orders within 14 days after we receive the returned goods or receive evidence that you have sent them back.

For eligible cancellations, we will refund the product price and the cost of standard delivery, if you paid for delivery. We may not refund any additional amount paid for express or premium delivery beyond the cost of standard delivery.

To request a return or cancellation, follow the instructions above under "How to start a return or cancellation."

Refunds

We'll notify you after receiving and inspecting your return and let you know whether the refund has been approved.

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method, unless otherwise agreed or required by law. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to process the refund.

If you haven't received an approved refund within a reasonable period, please contact us.

This policy does not limit your rights under applicable consumer protection laws, including rights relating to defective goods, incorrect descriptions, and other legal requirements.

Contact

For returns, cancellations, exchanges, damaged items, or other order questions, please contact us at hello@aokay.cool.
